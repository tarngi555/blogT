3 Thai Products to Help You Duel Spicy Food

You may have had Thai spicy food and found that they are too spicy than you expected. After that you struggle to find what to help you with that. Discover 3 Thai products here. Prepare them next time when you are about to have Thai dishes.

Local Thai Products & Expat Living

Thailand is the land of variety of food. Coming to this country to try Thai dishes is one of the list of many people. I assume yall may have tried some of them. Plus, someone, there'll be no next time for time since IT IS VERY SPICY!!! You probably ordered some Thai spicy dishes and have no idea how that will fire your mouth up. Or you just want to taste some that you already know what you are told. Thai dishes are something spicy even some Thais couldn't handle them. Anyway, they are delicious but they are just spicy.

For someone who's new for Thai dishes and accidentally have them and find it very hot, spicy. I have 3 Thai products that can help you cool your mouth in just a few minutes. They can be found at every corner of Bangkok. Let's get into some of them before your mouth all burned.

Coconut Water

This one is suitable after having spicy dishes. It can keep you hydrated because spicy food make you sweat. So, it can help you with the fluid you lose. Also, it provides recovering after you sweating. Thai Coconut water has variety of brands and price's range. However, it is in Thailand. It can be very easy to find and it is affordable. Go check to your 7‑Eleven nearby.

Thai Iced Milk Tea

This one is the most popular among Thai people. We love drinking iced milk tea. This one can help you after having Thai spicy food. Thai iced milk tea provides immediate relief from the heat. It helps cool you down and reduce heat sensation in your mouth. Its combination of cold tea, milk and sweetness make it easier to recover from the heat. Importantly, it is easy to drink, everybody loves sweetness. This one has potential. You should keep it with you when it comes to Thai spicy dishes. You should try it even if you are not eating spicy food. This one is something delicious. For this one, you can find it from cafe or coffee shops which mostly are located in markets nearby or along the street. Or just get into 7‑Eleven.

Plain Yogurt

This one is perfect for those who need healthy product but can solve the pain. A plain yogurt must be mentioned. It gives you relief without adding lot of sugar. Its creamy texture can make the burning sense feel less intense.
