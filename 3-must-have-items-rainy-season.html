3 Must-Have Items in Thailand Rainy Season

Survive in the rainy season in Thailand with 3 Must-have items. They are easy to find, just get into 7‑Eleven. Let's see what you can have today!!!

Local Thai Products & Expat Living

I'm pretty sure that you have been tired of raining lately every single day. Especially after work at the evening. It always is the rain right after you walk out of the building. You might have said "Again? at this time?" Not only just raining, there are floods every corner of the city. It is so hard to commute back to your place. That's sound horrible and you may have experienced them all. Anyway, we can't fight with it. We can handle it. In this site, you will discover promising items listed for this rainy season. Plus, they are easy to find and affordable. They are in 7‑Eleven or convenient stores. Let's get into and see what are listed for you!!!

Water Proof Shoes Cover

Everybody wears shoes every day to go outside. I do have my own. I love wearing sneakers. I have my favorite one. I don't want them to get wet or dirty. I'm sure you also get what I'm saying. However, we are facing with the super rainy season. Every time we get home after get through raining, our favorite shoes get wet. We need them to dry over night but sometimes they don't.

A water proof shoes cover can help what you are undergoing. It is design for agility feeling like you're not wearing them. There is a zip to protect your shoes from water to get inside. It is simple to use and has durable materials. Also, non-slip shoes sole is provided for your safety walking on the street while raining. With this shoes cover, you will never be worried about wearing your favorite shoes outside in the rainy season.

Moisture Absorber

After you went through heavily raining, your shirts, socks or clothes definitely get wet. Then you take them to laundry. They are so hard to dry because it rains every day. We have barely see sunlight lately. Even though they are successfully dry, there is gonna be some smell that is from your clothes taking some time to get dry. Plus, your closet hanging those clothes will be full of the bad smell. Have you ever tried of this?

Moisture Absorber will solve this relentless problem. It performs the best in the narrow space. It can absorb humidity that your clothes emit in the closet. It will spread the pleasant smell. You can choose which smell you'd like. Let go off the bad smell, replace it with the smell you like and enjoy.

Foot Odor Spray

In the rainy season, smell is the most thing to notice. Particularly, your shoes. Your shoes after it getting dry and you wear it, when you take it off. There is gonna be odor that is extremely bad. The humidity is still inside your shoes. Everybody around you or even you can simple smell it very musty. You must be so embarrassed if someone finds out. So, what can you do about it?

Foot Odor Spray can help you with that. Just use it when you are about to wear your shoes. Use it around each of the shoes and then inside. Wait for a sec to wear it. It can prevent the bad smell throughout the day. It is simply portable. You can take it to your workplace, making sure your feet and shoes' smell good enough.
